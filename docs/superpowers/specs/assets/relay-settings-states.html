Relay

Agents that run in the cloud cannot reach this computer when it is asleep or on a different network. A relay holds their updates until calico is back. It runs on your own Cloudflare account, on the free plan.

What this does

calico deploys a small Worker to your Cloudflare account and keeps two secret tokens: a send token for your agents and a read token that stays here. Nothing about your account is sent to the calico project.

Two buttons, not a wizard: the common case first, and "connect an existing relay" is the same screen for anyone running a self-hosted ntfy. The disclosure is closed by default, so the section stays short.

Relay

Before anything happens, here is exactly what calico will do:

  1. Open your browser so you can sign in to Cloudflare. calico never sees your password.
  2. Ask for one permission, Workers Admin. It is the smallest one Cloudflare has that allows uploading a Worker.
  3. Deploy a Worker called calico-relay to your account, with two secret tokens.
  4. Check that it answers, then revoke its own access.

The relay stays in your account. You can delete it there at any time.

The same inline confirm the webhook-token replacement already uses (amber border, no modal). DESIGN.md says to show what is about to happen before anything privileged. The permission is named in the app's words first, because Cloudflare's consent screen will say "Workers Admin".
